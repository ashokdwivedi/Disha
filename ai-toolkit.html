Future-Ready Toolkit

Free AI Tools & Online Courses

Real learning no longer waits for a classroom. Here are trusted, free resources for every stage of the journey — organised by age, not by subject.

Ages 6–13

Primary & Middle School

DIKSHA

Govt. of India's national platform with free NCERT-aligned textbooks, videos and practice content for every grade.

Khan Academy

Completely free video lessons and practice for maths, science and reading, from basics upward.

Duolingo

A free, game-like way to build English or another language, useful for exams and confidence later.

Scratch

A free, visual block-based coding tool from MIT — a fun first step into programming logic.

Ages 14–18

Secondary School & Stream Choice

SWAYAM

India's national MOOC platform — free courses with certificates, great for previewing a subject before you commit to a stream.

NPTEL

Free introductory engineering and science modules from IITs/IISc — a genuine early look at what each field involves.

National Career Service (NCS)

A government portal for career guidance, aptitude information and job-market awareness.

Ages 18+

Higher Education & Career

NPTEL & SWAYAM

Full degree-level courses with certificates, free — a genuine alternative or supplement to expensive coaching.

Coursera

Audit almost any course for free (no certificate) from top global universities and companies.

Using AI assistants as a study partner

AI tools are excellent for explaining a tough concept a different way, practising interview questions, or getting feedback on writing — used as a study aid, not a shortcut around learning or exams.
