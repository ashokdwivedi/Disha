Crossover Careers

Some careers don't care which stream you came from. Whether you studied Science, Commerce or Arts, these three paths stay genuinely open.

  1. from Science from Commerce from Arts

    How to get there

    Any bachelor's degree + a B.Ed (Bachelor of Education) qualifies you to teach that subject in schools. Some universities also offer integrated 4-year B.A./B.Sc.-B.Ed programmes.

    Free resources

    • DIKSHA — content and training for teachers, govt. of India
    • NISHTHA (via SWAYAM) — national teacher-training programme
  2. from Science from Commerce from Arts

    How to get there

    Any graduate, from any stream, can sit the UPSC Civil Services Exam (Prelims → Mains → Interview) for IAS, IPS, IFS and other services. State Public Service Commissions (PSCs) run similar exams.

    Free resources

    • UPSC official site — syllabus, notifications and past papers
    • SWAYAM — history, polity, economy & geography courses
  3. from Science from Commerce from Arts

    How to get there

    No fixed degree path — engineers build products, commerce graduates handle finance/operations, and arts graduates often lead brand, content or people functions. Many founding teams mix all three.

    Free resources

    • Startup India — govt. resources, registration & schemes
    • Coursera — audit business-fundamentals courses for free
