Crossover Map

Streams are starting points, not destinations

Real students regularly move sideways from their stream. Here are common, genuine crossovers:

From Science

→Design (via UCEED / NID)
→Civil Services (UPSC — open to all graduates)
→Data Science & Analytics
→Teaching (B.Ed after graduation)

From Commerce

→Analytics & Data (via statistics, Excel, SQL)
→Law (5-year integrated LLB via CLAT)
→Design & Entrepreneurship
→Teaching (Commerce & Economics)

From Arts

→Civil Services (a natural fit, very common path)
→Design (via NID / NIFT)
→Digital Media & Content Careers
→Teaching (a very natural next step)

Notice the pattern: Teaching and Civil Services are reachable from every stream. See the full detail on the Crossover Careers page.

A Fourth Path

Vocational & Skill-Based Education

Not every student needs a traditional degree first. ITIs (Industrial Training Institutes) and Polytechnics offer focused, practical training leading directly into a trade or a diploma-level engineering role — often faster and more affordable.

It also connects horizontally: a Polytechnic diploma holder can later join a BTech via lateral entry, and skilled tradespeople increasingly move into entrepreneurship.

Free resource: Skill India Digital (skillindiadigital.gov.in) lists government-recognised skill courses and certifications.
