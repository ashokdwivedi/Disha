Classes 1–10 · Ages 6–15

Building the Foundation

Before choosing a stream, every student passes through primary, middle and secondary school. This is the time to build strong basics and explore widely — not to specialise too early.

The Roadmap

Three stages before the big choice

  1. Where this leads

    Ages 6–10, following NCERT/CBSE, ICSE or State Board syllabi. Leads directly into Middle School (Classes 6–8).

    Explore & discover

    • Try art, music, sports, storytelling and simple logic/puzzle games — variety matters more than depth right now.
    • Playful introductions to computers and typing build comfort with technology early.

    Free resources

    • DIKSHA — Govt. of India's free NCERT-aligned digital content
    • Khan Academy — free early maths and reading practice
    • Duolingo — fun, free language learning

  2. Where this leads

    Ages 11–13. Subjects separate into distinct Maths, Science and Social Science — leads into Secondary School (Classes 9–10).

    Explore & discover

    • Notice which subjects you enjoy — this is useful early data for stream choice later, not a decision yet.
    • Try a first taste of coding (block-based tools) and small science experiments at home or school.

    Free resources

    • DIKSHA & NCERT e-books
    • Scratch — free, beginner-friendly visual coding
    • Khan Academy — maths & science practice by grade

  3. Where this leads

    Ages 14–15, ending in the Class 10 board exam (CBSE/ICSE/State Board) — leads directly into choosing a stream.

    Explore & discover

    • Talk to seniors, teachers and family in different streams — ask what a normal week actually looks like for them.
    • Use free aptitude/career-awareness tools, but treat them as a starting conversation, not a final verdict.

    Free resources

    • National Career Service (NCS) — govt. career guidance portal
    • SWAYAM — free structured courses to preview subjects
    • Khan Academy — board-exam-aligned revision
