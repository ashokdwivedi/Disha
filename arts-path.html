Arts / Humanities Stream

Design, Law, Civil Services & More

History, Political Science, Psychology and Literature build sharp thinking and communication — the base for some of India's most sought-after careers.

  1. Where this leads

    Design: UCEED/NID/NIFT entrance → BDes → product, fashion or communication design. Law: CLAT → 5-year integrated LLB → litigation, corporate law or judiciary.

    Or pivot to…

    • UX/product design in tech companies
    • Policy & legal research
    • Civil Services (law graduates do very well in UPSC)

    Free resources

    • Coursera — audit design-fundamentals & intro-law courses
    • SWAYAM — legal studies & design MOOCs

  2. Where this leads

    UPSC Civil Services (Prelims/Mains/Interview) is a very natural fit for Arts graduates due to overlapping syllabus. Psychology degrees lead to counselling, clinical practice (with further study) or HR.

    Or pivot to…

    • Public policy & social work
    • Human resources & organisational behaviour
    • Teaching (a very common and natural next step)

    Free resources

    • SWAYAM — political science, public administration & psychology courses
    • Khan Academy — free social-science & psychology basics

  3. Where this leads

    BA in Journalism/Mass Communication → newsrooms, broadcast, or the fast-growing digital content and social-media industry.

    Or pivot to…

    • Content strategy & digital marketing
    • Corporate communications
    • Independent creator / entrepreneurship

    Free resources

    • Coursera — audit writing & digital-marketing courses
    • Duolingo — language skills for global communication roles
