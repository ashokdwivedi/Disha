Science Stream

Engineering, Medicine & Research

Physics, Chemistry, Maths (PCM) or Biology (PCB) open three big vertical paths — and several genuine horizontal ones too.

  1. Where this leads

    JEE Main / JEE Advanced → NITs, IITs and state engineering colleges → BTech → core engineering roles, software, or higher studies (MTech / MS abroad).

    Or pivot to…

    • Product management & UX design
    • Civil Services (UPSC — open to any degree)
    • Data Science & Entrepreneurship

    Free resources

    • NPTEL — free full-length engineering courses from IITs/IISc
    • SWAYAM — govt. MOOCs with certificates
    • Coursera — audit intro CS/data-science courses for free

  2. Where this leads

    NEET → MBBS / BDS, or AYUSH options (BAMS/BHMS) → internship → General Practice or specialisation (MD/MS).

    Or pivot to…

    • Public health administration
    • Medical research
    • Health-tech entrepreneurship & medical teaching (faculty)

    Free resources

    • Khan Academy — free biology & health/medicine fundamentals
    • SWAYAM — life-science and health courses
    • NPTEL — biotechnology modules

  3. Where this leads

    BSc (Physics/Chemistry/Maths/Biology) → MSc → PhD → Research (IISc, TIFR, CSIR labs) or academia.

    Or pivot to…

    • Data Science & Analytics
    • Science communication & journalism
    • Civil Services and EdTech content creation

    Free resources

    • NPTEL & SWAYAM — full science degree-level courses, free
    • Coursera — audit research-methods courses
