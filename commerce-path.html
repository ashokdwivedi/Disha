Commerce Stream

Finance, Business & Analytics

Accountancy, Business Studies and Economics build the base for professional qualifications, management roles, and a growing pivot into data-driven careers.

  1. Where this leads

    Professional exams via ICAI (CA), ICSI (CS) or ICMAI (CMA) — can be started right after Class 12 alongside a BCom, leading to auditing, taxation, company law or cost management roles.

    Or pivot to…

    • Corporate law & compliance
    • Financial consulting or CFO track
    • Entrepreneurship (deep finance knowledge helps founders)

    Free resources

    • SWAYAM — accountancy & taxation MOOCs
    • Khan Academy — free accounting & economics basics

  2. Where this leads

    BCom / BBA / BMS → banking, financial services, or an MBA for management roles; CFA for investment/finance specialisation.

    Or pivot to…

    • Civil Services (UPSC/state PSCs)
    • Design & brand management
    • Teaching (Commerce & Economics, via B.Ed)

    Free resources

    • Coursera — audit financial-markets & management courses
    • SWAYAM — business & economics degree-level courses

  3. Where this leads

    Commerce graduates who pick up statistics, Excel and SQL are strong candidates for business analyst and data-analytics roles — no engineering degree required.

    Or pivot to…

    • Business intelligence / MIS roles
    • Product analytics in startups
    • Further study: PG Diploma in Data Science

    Free resources

    • NPTEL — free intro-programming & statistics modules
    • Coursera — audit Excel/SQL/data-analytics courses
